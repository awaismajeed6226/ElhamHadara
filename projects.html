Key Projects

1. Cox Energy – Maaden Phosphate 1, Turaif, Saudi Arabia

Project Title: Auxiliary Steel Structures & 65 MW Gas Turbine Package Works

Scope of Work:

  • Fabrication and erection of auxiliary steel structures and industrial platforms.
  • Installation of 65 MW gas turbine package pipe rack systems.
  • Mechanical piping works associated with gas turbine systems.
  • Fabrication and erection of structural steel for transformer areas including platforms and roofing structures.
  • Structural steel works including beams, angles, grating platforms, handrails, ladders, and access systems.

2. Al Zamil Cool Care – Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (Multiple Locations)

Project Title: Solar PV Installation Program for Government Schools

Locations: Northern Borders (Turaif, Arar, Al Uwayqilah), Al Ahsa, Dammam, Madinah

Scope of Work:

  • Turnkey installation of 15.2 kW rooftop solar PV systems for government schools.
  • Electrical installation works including power distribution and system integration.
  • Mechanical installation of PV mounting structures.
  • Low voltage cabling and data communication wiring works.
  • Testing, commissioning, and handover across large-scale school rollout programs.
